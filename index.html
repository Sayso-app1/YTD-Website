THE PROBLEM

3:14 a.m. on I-70.
Sixty seconds nobody is watching.

Fatigue doesn't show up on a logbook. It builds quietly over a long clock, and by the time a camera or a scorecard notices, the damage is done. Drivers feel it first, and nothing in the cab is listening.

HOUR TENOf a fourteen-hour clock.

THE RUMBLE STRIPCatches him. This time.

Fatigue costs twice: once in crashes, again in the drivers who quit.

THE PLATFORM

An app, plus the wearable already on their wrist.

No cameras, no audio, nothing bolted to the truck. YouTheDriver runs on the driver's own phone and watch, so it goes wherever they go.

PREEMPT Predicts fatigue from the wrist, before the drift. Sleep, heart rate and time since the last break roll into one live Green / Yellow / Red status, with a clear next step.
REWARD Pays drivers for driving rested. Points, badges and real rewards for safe, healthy habits, spent on the things drivers actually need on the road.
CONNECT Ends 250 nights a year alone. Live parking, showers and fuel, plus peer intel from drivers in the same fleet and region.
ADVOCATE Turns driver concerns into evidence. Log an issue by voice in seconds. YTD rolls it into reports that fleets can act on.
HOW IT WORKS

Rolling in one shift.

Nothing to install in the cab. The fleet enrolls, drivers pair their watch, and the app takes it from there.

MILE1 Fleet enrolls A 15-minute enrollment session for the whole fleet.
MILE2 Pair the watch Drivers connect the wearable they already own in about two minutes.
MILE3 Check in A quick fatigue self-check before the shift starts.
MILE4 Drive with a heads-up Green, Yellow or Red in real time, with a break or rest stop suggested early.
MILE5 Earn Rested starts and on-time breaks add up to points and rewards.

Healthier drivers stay on the road longer.
